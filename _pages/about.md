Hello! My name is Ruijie Fu, and I am a fourth-year PhD student in the [School of Automation and Intelligent Sensing](https://sais.sjtu.edu.cn/) and the [John Hopcroft Center for Computer Science](https://jhc.sjtu.edu.cn/) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Prior to that, I received my bachelor's degree from Jilin University in 2022. My research interests are mainly in the __control, communication, and computation co-design__ for Cyber-Physical Systems and Real-time Systems. You can find my CV [here](https://github.com/Jeffery22-Sjtu/jeffery22.github.io/tree/master/files/Ruijie_Fu_s_CV.pdf).
